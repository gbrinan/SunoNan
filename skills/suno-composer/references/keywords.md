# Suno Keyword Dictionary

Reference data for the inference steps in SKILL.md. Load when choosing vocabulary; pick few, precise terms over many.

## 1. Genres & styles

**Mainstream:** Pop, Rock, Jazz, EDM, Lo-fi, Country, Metal, Indie, Trap, Folk, Ambient, Blues, Hip-hop, R&B, Reggae, Orchestral, Cinematic, Funk, Synthwave, Disco, Gospel, Ballad, Classical, Dance-pop, House, Trance, Dubstep, Experimental, Alternative, Techno, Progressive, Chillwave, Neo-soul, Alt-pop, Acoustic, Ska, Swing, Musical, New Age

**Rock & metal derivatives:**
- Punk Rock - simple raw chords, rebellious attitude
- Grunge - 90s alternative rock (Nirvana style)
- Shoegaze - heavy guitar effects, dreamy wall of noise
- Post-Rock - rhythm/texture-driven instrumental
- Math Rock - complex meters, technical
- Emo - confessional lyrics over punk/rock
- Nu Metal - hip-hop + punk hybrid metal
- Psychedelic Rock - 60s-70s hallucinatory style
- Soft Rock - comfortable, melodic, mainstream
- Gothic Rock - dark, dramatic
- Industrial - mechanical, aggressive
- Pop Punk - pop melody + punk energy
- Power Metal - grand, epic
- Metalcore - metal + hardcore punk
- Britpop - 90s UK guitar pop

**Electronic (deep):**
- Drum and Bass (DnB) - very fast complex breakbeats
- UK Garage / 2-Step - shuffle rhythm, vocal cut-ups
- IDM - listening-oriented, experimental
- Hardstyle - punishing kick, distorted sound
- Glitch Hop - digital error sounds as rhythm
- Tropical House - bright, refreshing house
- Future Bass - dreamy synths + heavy bass
- Eurobeat - fast tempo, synth melody
- Electro Swing - vintage swing + electronic
- Glitch Pop - pop + digital glitch

**Hip-hop & urban:**
- Soul - gospel+R&B, deep emotional vocals
- Motown - 60s pop soul
- Drill - dark sliding-bass hip-hop
- Grime - UK fast rough electronic hip-hop
- Boom Bap - 90s East Coast gritty drums
- New Jack Swing - 80s-90s dance beat + R&B
- Rage - game-like synths + heavy bass
- Pluggnb - dreamy synths + R&B vocals + rap
- Jersey Drill - drill beat + Jersey club rhythm

**World & Latin:** K-Pop, J-Pop / City Pop, Reggaeton, Bossa Nova, Samba, Salsa, Afrobeats, Bachata, Celtic, Trot, Latin Pop / Urbano, Amapiano, Dancehall, Dub, Bluegrass

**Internet-native & short-form:** Hyperpop, Phonk / Drift Phonk, Glitchcore, Sped Up, Sigilkore, Jersey Club, Baltimore Club, Breakcore

**Mood & aesthetic genres:** Vaporwave, Dream Pop, Bedroom Pop, Weirdcore / Dreamcore, Trip-hop, Art Pop, Baroque Pop, Chamber Pop, Darkwave, Future Funk, Yacht Rock, Acid Jazz, Shibuya-kei

## 2. Instruments & production

- **Strings:** acoustic guitar, electric guitar, violin, cello, strings, harp, ukulele, bass
- **Keys:** piano, electric piano, organ, synth, retro synths, electronic pads
- **Percussion:** drums, percussion, cinematic drums, marimba, xylophone, glockenspiel, vibraphone, handpan, rain drum
- **Winds:** flute, saxophone, brass, trumpet
- **Electronic:** 808 bass, EDM beats, ambient textures, sound effects, loops
- **Vocal layers:** choir backing
- **Orchestra:** orchestral elements

**Production reference phrases (verified):**
- "recorded in a basement studio" - lo-fi/indie texture
- "Phil Spector wall of sound" - thick wall-like sound
- "lo-fi tape recording" - vintage tape texture
- "vinyl crackle" - vinyl noise
- "cathedral reverb" - cathedral-scale reverb
- "wide stereo ambience" - wide stereo space

- **Mode:** spoken word, rap, character voice, persona-driven, animated, theatrical

## 7. Combination formula

`[Major/Minor] + [Genre] + [Tempo] + [Instruments+Vocal] + [Mood]`

Verified sample:

> Minor, Lo-fi hiphop, 60bpm, Melancholic piano keys with vinyl crackle sound, Whispery female vocals, Nostalgic atmosphere, Late night vibes

Suno weights earlier elements more heavily - put the most important element first.
